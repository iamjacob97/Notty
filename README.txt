Notty
Notty is a strategic card game designed for 2 to 3 players, involving number and colour matching with a twist of open-hand gameplay and unpredictable card draws.

Each turn presents meaningful decisions—draw, steal, or discard—but planning and observation are key to winning.

🃏 Game Overview
Deck Composition:

80 cards total

Each card has:

A colour: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow

A number: 1 through 10

Each (colour, number) combination appears exactly twice in the deck

🎮 Setup
Shuffle the deck thoroughly.

Deal 5 cards to each player.

All players place their cards face up, i.e, hands are visible to everyone.

The remaining deck stays face down and forms the draw pile.

🔁 Gameplay
Players take turns clockwise. On a turn, a player may do the following actions in any order:

🔹 Draw Cards (Max thrice per turn)
Draw up to 3 cards from the face-down deck.

The player does not see drawn cards until they've finished drawing.

Once finished, the drawn cards are revealed.

🔹 Steal a Card (Once per turn)
Choose another player and take one random card from their hand.

The target player shuffles their hand face down, and the stealing player picks blindly.

🔹 Discard Groups (Any number of times per turn)
A valid group must be one of:

Sequence

At least 3 cards of the same colour. Numbers must be consecutive.

Example: Blue 4, Blue 5, Blue 6

Set

At least 3 cards of the same number. All cards must have different colours.

Example: Red 4, Blue 4, Green 4

❌ Duplicates like Blue 4, Red 4, Blue 4 are invalid

Players may also pass (i.e., take no actions) on their turn.

🔄 Discarding & Deck Recycling
Whenever cards are discarded, they are added back into the deck

The entire deck is reshuffled

This ensures that used cards can re-enter the game, keeping every round unpredictable.

🏆 Win Condition
The first player to discard all their cards is the winner.
