--------------------------- Game Instructions ------------------------
These instructions outline the key rules for playing the Notty card game.
1. Once per turn, a player can draw up to 3 cards from the deck.  
   - The player does not need to decide on the number of cards to draw in advance.
   - The drawn cards remain face down until the drawing is complete.

2. Once per turn, a player can choose another player and take a random card from their hand.
   - The chosen player must shuffle their hand face-down and allow the current player to pick one card randomly.

3. A player may discard a valid group of cards any number of times per turn. 
   A valid group must be one of the following types:
   - A sequence of at least three cards of the same color with consecutive numbers (e.g., blue 4, blue 5, blue 6).
   - A set of at least three cards with the same number but different colors (e.g., blue 4, green 4, red 4). 
     Note: Repeated colors are not allowed in this group type.
